City & Mohalla Ecosystem - Ultimate Blueprint

A highly detailed breakdown of user features, election simulations, and admin controls.

Tech Stack: Flutter (Frontend) | Django (Backend API & Admin Panel) | Firebase (Real-time DB & FCM)

0. Legal & App Store Compliance (CRITICAL)

Voter Slip Policy

Strict Directive: Google Play Store aur Apple App Store ki government impersonation guidelines ko follow karte hue, "Digital Voter Slip" ya kisi bhi tarah ka fake voter ID feature app se puri tarah aur hamesha ke liye remove kar diya gaya he. Ye feature na abhi aayega aur na hi kisi future release me add kiya jayega. Voting puri tarah se app ke internal digital secure poll system par aadharit hogi.

1. User Authentication & Mohalla Assignment

User Flow (Flutter App)

  • Signup Via Google: 1-Tap secure login. No fake manual emails.
  • Device ID Binding: Ek phone se sirf ek account ki vote register hogi (Fraud prevention).
  • Mohalla Selection: Profile banate waqt user dropdown se apni city aur Mohalla select karega, aur turant wahan add ho jayega.
  • No Self-Change: Ek bar mohalla set hone ke baad user khud change nahi kar sakta. Change karne ke liye Django Admin ko "Request" dalni hogi, jise admin verify karke approve/decline karega.

Admin & Ecosystem Link

  • Mohalla Freeze: Jab chunav date announce hogi, sabhi Mohalla change requests automatically block/freeze ho jayengi.
  • City Unique ID: Har city ka apna alag independent ecosystem hoga (e.g., Kandhla ki alag feed aur database tables).

2. Mohalla Feed, Interactions & Concerns

Within Own Mohalla

  • Post Types: Text, Photos, aur Polls (Video abhi nahi hogi).
  • Concerns (Samasya): User issue ki photo aur details dekar Concern raise karega. Log us par "Support" ya "Do Not Support" par interact karenge.
  • Like/Dislike: Har post par Like aur Dislike ka option hoga.
  • Credibility Score: Users aur Leaders ko mile Likes, Support aur kiye gaye achhe kaamon se unki Credibility badhegi. Minus (Dislikes) aane par kam hogi. Ye score chunav me parcha bharne ke liye matter karega.

Cross-Mohalla Interactions

  • View Access: User dropdown se dusre mohalle select karke dekh payega ki wahan kya chal raha he.
  • Limited Interaction: Wahan jakar Like, Dislike, Support, Do Not Support kar sakega, lekin Comment nahi kar sakega (Aapsi takrav rokne ke liye).
  • Anonymity: Dusre mohalle me dikhega ki "15 Likes from Mohalla X", lekin kis user ne kiye, unke naam show nahi honge.

3. The City Election (Supreme Minister - Har 3 Maheene me)

Puri city ka maha-chunav jiska aayojan City Election Commission (Admin Panel) karega.

Exact 15-Day Timeline Flow

  • Day 1 to 5 (Parcha/Nomination): Chunav aayog date nikalega. Har mohalle se log parcha bhar sakenge. 5 din baad form link auto-close.
  • Day 6 (Allocation): Admin panel se verify karke top 30 logo ko "Seats and Symbols" allocate kiye jayenge.
  • Day 6 to 10 (Prachar): Candidates app me apna zor-dar prachar-prasar karenge.
  • Day 11 (Achaar Sanhita): Pure app par Achaar Sanhita lag jayegi. Koi naya post ya prachar nahi hoga. App lock mode me jayegi.
  • Day 12 & 13 (Voting): 2 din tak voting chalegi. 1 Aadmi = 1 Vote. Secret ballot system.
  • Day 14 (Counting & Result): Achaar Sanhita hategi, post karna shuru hoga. Counting hogi aur app par naye Supreme Minister ka result sabke samne aayega.
  • Day 15 (Oath & Cabinet): Naya Supreme Minister Oath lega. Wo apni cabinet banayega jisme adhiktam 11 log ho sakenge.

4. The Mohalla Election (Har Maheene)

Mohalle ki local sarkar. Parcha sirf usi mohalle ke log bhar sakenge.

Exact 11-Day Timeline Flow

  • Day 1 to 2 (Parcha): Sirf 2 din parcha bharne ka time.
  • Day 3 (Allocation): Symbols aur Seats allocate hongi.
  • Day 4 to 8 (Prachar): 5 din tak mohalle me prachar.
  • Day 9 (Achaar Sanhita): Sirf usi mohalle me achaar sanhita lagegi aur feed lock hogi.
  • Day 10 (Voting, Counting & Result): Ek hi din me votes dalengi, counting hogi aur result jari hoga.
  • Day 11 (Oath & Cabinet): Mohalla Minister ki Oath hogi aur wo apni cabinet me 5 log select karega.

6. Privileges & Detailed Cabinet Departments

👑 Supreme Minister (City Leader) Privileges

  • God Mode View: Kisi bhi mohalle me post, comment, like aur interact kar sakega.
  • Give Orders: Kisi bhi mohalle ko direct "Orders" de payega jo us mohalle me highlight honge.
  • Flagging/Shadow Ban Request: Kisi bhi user ko Flag ya Shadow ban karne ki direct power. (Report admin panel me jayegi, Admin ka faisla antim hoga. Admin accept karke restrict ya decline kar sakta he).

🛡️ Supreme Cabinet (11 Members) - Violet Ruby

1. Grah Mantri (Home Minister)

Privilege: Moderation ka sabse bada adhikari. Kisi ki 1st strike (6 hours shadow ban) ko directly approve karna. Hate speech reports ko priority par dekhna.

Privilege: Har mohalle se aane wale "Concerns" filter karna. Valid concerns ko "City Priority" tag dekar highlight karna.

Privilege: City ke sabhi mohallo me "Official Announcements" broadcast karna aur Push Notifications shoot karna.

4. Kanun Mantri (Law & Justice Minister)

Privilege: Banned users ki appeals ko review karna. City Samvidhan ke rules enforce karwana.

5. Jan-Shikayat Mantri (Grievance Minister)

Privilege: Cross-mohalla disputes handle karna. Takrav karne wale users ko special warning dena.

6. Sanskritik Mantri (Cultural & Events Minister)

Privilege: Pure city ke liye special mega-polls aur digital competitions create karna.

7. Swasthya evam Safai Mantri (Health & Sanitation)

Privilege: Kachre/bimariyo se jude concerns ko red-alert tag lagana.

8. Shiksha evam Yuva Mantri (Education & Youth)

Privilege: Students aur youth se judi jankariyo aur posts ko city feed me upar rank karwana.

9. Nagar Suraksha Mantri (Safety & Vigilance)

Privilege: App me chal rahe spam ya scam posts ko ek click me hide (takedown) karna (Admin review ke liye).

10. Vitt Mantri (Finance Minister)

Privilege: App par chalne wale local ads (Jaise Janta Dry Clean) ki performance aur transparency reports dekhna.

11. Takniki Mantri (IT & App Minister)

Privilege: Bugs ya feature suggestions ko direct Master Admin/Developer ke dashboard me push karna.

🟢 Mohalla Cabinet (5 Members) - Grey Ruby

1. Up-Mantri (Deputy Mohalla Minister)

Privilege: Agar Mohalla Minister active nahi he ya banned he, to mohalle ki primary feed aur control manage karna.

2. Mohalla Vikas Prabhari (Infra Head)

Privilege: Mohalle ki "Concerns" ki sachai verify karna aur genuine hone par City Vikas Mantri ko forward karna.

3. Mohalla Shanti Prabhari (Moderation Head)

Privilege: Mohalle me gaali-galoch ya ladai wali posts ko flag karke seedha City Grah Mantri ko report bhejna.

Privilege: Mohalle ki top feed me local news, shadi cards, ya chhote announcements pin karna.

5. Mohalla Jan-Sampark (Community Head)

Privilege: Naye users ke aane par automatic welcome post generate karna aur local polls chalana.

7. Moderation, Strike System & Admin Panels

3-Strike Rule & Filtering

  • Blocked Words: Admin panel me words ki list hogi. User wo gali/word type karega to post nahi hogi.
  • Abusive Behavior Penalties:
    1. Pehli baar: 6 Ghante Shadow Ban (Reason ke sath).
    2. Dusri baar: 24 Ghante Ban.
    3. Teesri baar: 3 Din Ban.
    4. Chauthi baar: Permanent Ban. Us user ko mohalle me "Featured Banned Profile" dikhaya jayega as an example.

Master Admin vs City Election Commission

  • Master Admin: Nayi cities, colonies add, update aur delete karega (CRUD). Admin se direct kisi bhi mohalle me global post daal sakega (Ad ya Announcement) jo sabse top par pin hogi.
  • City Admin (Election Commission): Ye har city ka apna independent panel hoga. Ye chunav ki dates dalenge, application approve/disapprove karenge, symbols and seats denge. Voting management aur counting inke panel me hogi.

8. Premium City Samvidhan & Notifications

Targeted Notifications (FCM) & Ads

  • Monetization: Admin kisi bhi mohalle me top par local ads (Jaise Janta Dry Clean, Laundry, etc.) pin kar sakega aur exact impressions/clicks track karega.
  • FCM Targeting: Admin ya Cabinet se bheje gaye push notifications target kiye ja sakenge: Ya to puri city ko, ya sirf ek specific mohalle ko, ya kisi ek specific user ko.

Samvidhan System (Backend)

  • Har city ka ek Samvidhan hoga jise Admin panel ke rich text editor se update kiya jayega.
  • Naye user ko is samvidhan par "I pledge to abide by the City Samvidhan" agree karke hi app me entry milegi.
